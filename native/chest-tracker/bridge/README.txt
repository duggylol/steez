Steez Chest Bridge
==================

For a two-PC setup: this counts your Fortnite chests on your GAME PC and sends them to Steez on
your STREAM PC, over your home network.

1. On your stream PC: open Steez > Fortnite Chests > "Where's Fortnite?" > choose "Another PC".
2. On your game PC: put SteezChestBridge.exe anywhere (Desktop, Documents...) and open it.
   Nothing to install. It has no window: look for the chest icon in the tray (by the clock;
   it may be under the ^ arrow).
3. Back in Steez, your game PC shows up with an Allow button. Click it. Done - it remembers.

It starts with Windows and reconnects by itself. Right-click the tray icon to pause counting,
turn off "Start with Windows", or quit.

Light on your game PC: it runs at low priority and only reads the screen while a Fortnite match
is on (to catch the "+XP CHEST" popup), and reads the match's replay when it ends. Turn on
Settings > Game > Replays > Record Replays in Fortnite for exact counts, teammates' chests and
chests you break.

Can't find Steez? Right-click the tray icon > "Connect to Steez by address" and type the address
shown on the Fortnite Chests page in Steez. If Windows on the stream PC asked whether Steez may
use the network, it needs to be allowed on private networks.

Windows may warn the first time you open it ("Windows protected your PC") because the app isn't
signed: click "More info" > "Run anyway".
